How OmniStream Processes Media

STEP 01

Stream Probing

yt-dlp inspects remote adaptive DASH & HLS video manifests without triggering rate limits.

STEP 02

H.264 Synthesis

Video is filtered for AVC/H.264 profiles while AAC 192k stereo is extracted for native Windows/QuickTime playback.

STEP 03

FFmpeg Local Mux

Isolated threads stream directly to disk and mux the container with zero re-encoding loss in seconds.

Responsible Use & Fair Use Guidance

OmniStream is engineered as a local personal utility for content creators, researchers, and offline backup storage. You should only download media when you have obtained permission from the copyright owner, when the work is distributed under an open license (such as Creative Commons), or where your use falls cleanly within the Fair Use or Fair Dealing statutory exemptions of your jurisdiction.

Do not use this software to distribute copyrighted commercial works, bypass DRM encryption, or violate third-party Terms of Service.

Open-Source Attributions

OmniStream is powered by world-class open-source projects:

  • yt-dlp: Command-line audio/video downloader for hundreds of video sites.
  • FFmpeg: Complete, cross-platform solution to record, convert, and stream audio and video.
  • Flask: Lightweight WSGI web application framework for local Python services.
